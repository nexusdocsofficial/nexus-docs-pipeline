Russia is the biggest country on Earth, and its coast touches three oceans. Yet for three hundred years it has been desperate for one thing: a port that doesn't freeze, and a sea it can actually sail out of.

In this video we follow that obsession from Peter the Great's frozen window on the Baltic to Catherine the Great's Crimea, the Crimean War, Gorchakov's bold gamble, and the Baltic Fleet that sailed eighteen thousand miles only to be destroyed at Tsushima. Then we look at the warm door Russia found in the Arctic, why many historians say the 'warm water' story is too simple, and how those same doors are slamming shut again today: the closed Bosporus, a Black Sea fleet under attack, a Baltic Sea surrounded by NATO, the shadow fleet, the loss of Tartus, and the melting Northern Sea Route.
